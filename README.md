### 👋 About Me

I'm a fourth-year **Computer Science &amp; Business Systems** undergraduate at **Thapar Institute of Engineering &amp; Technology**, working at the intersection of data, operations, and business strategy. I build things that sit *between* a spreadsheet and a boardroom — dashboards, financial models, and reporting layers that a founder or ops lead can actually act on.

I gravitate toward ambiguous, process-heavy problems: the kind with no clean spec, where you have to reverse-engineer the workflow, define the metric, *and* build the tool. My work spans:

- 📊 **Data &amp; Financial Analysis** — DCF valuation, incentive/payout modeling, SQL-backed exception logic, root-cause variance analysis
- 📈 **Growth &amp; Marketing Analytics** — influencer ROI, creator segmentation, CPM/CPE optimization, GTM strategy
- 🏗️ **Business Systems &amp; Ops** — trade lifecycle tooling, SLA/exception monitoring, budget variance tracking
- 🎯 **Outreach &amp; Leadership** — large-scale fest sponsorship, cross-functional team coordination, stakeholder pitching

**Open to:** Founder's Office · Business/Data Analyst · BizOps · Strategy &amp; Consulting · Product &amp; Growth · Research &amp; Project roles — anywhere data needs to become a decision.
